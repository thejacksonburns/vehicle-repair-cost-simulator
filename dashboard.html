How the model works

Each available vehicle has a daily failure probability. A failed vehicle joins a first-in, first-out queue. Repairs require one crew and one spare part. Repair duration varies by ±1 day. Completed repairs return the next morning.

Spare orders restore inventory position to the target and arrive after the specified delivery delay. Inventory position includes parts already ordered. Vehicles do not fail during repair.

Costs include all crew-days, initial spare purchases, and replenishment orders placed—even orders that arrive after the simulation ends. Downtime, equipment acquisition, and residual inventory value are excluded.
